# Tenote Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

This policy covers the Tenote app distributed through the Mac App Store by Noemi Titarenco.

## Data collection

Tenote does not collect or send your notes, images, personal information, usage analytics, or advertising identifiers to the developer. It has no accounts, advertising, or tracking services.

## Notes and local storage

Your notes and pasted images are saved as files in the folder you choose. Settings and diagnostic logs are stored locally on your Mac. Logs may contain file paths and error details; they are not automatically uploaded. If you choose to share a log when requesting support, review it first for information you do not want to disclose.

You can edit, back up, or delete your note files using Finder or another application. Removing Tenote does not automatically remove notes from the folder you chose.

## Network access and third parties

If a note displays an image hosted on an external website, loading that image contacts that website. Its operator may receive ordinary request information, such as your IP address, under its own privacy practices. Images stored locally do not require this network request.

If you put your notes in an iCloud Drive, Dropbox, or other synchronized folder, the provider you chose handles those files under its own terms and privacy policy. Tenote does not operate a cloud storage service.

Opening support or privacy links opens the relevant website, which has its own privacy practices. Information you post to a public GitHub issue is public. Apple may separately process App Store or operating-system diagnostics under Apple's privacy policy and your settings.

## Permissions and bundled features

The Mac App Store app is sandboxed. It requests access to the notes folder you select and remembers that permission for future launches. Its commands, shortcuts, and themes are included in the app; this edition does not download or install external plugins. Any macOS permission prompt is governed by your choice in the system interface.

## Changes and contact

Material changes to this policy will be reflected here with an updated effective date. For privacy questions, contact the developer through [Tenote's support page](https://github.com/noemit/tenote/issues). Do not include private note contents or sensitive personal information in a public issue.
